1. ADOPTION AND ADOPTION FEE
The Rescue agrees to place the cat identified above (the "Cat") into the Adopter's care, and the Adopter agrees to accept the Cat subject to the terms of this Agreement.
The adoption fee is $150.00, due at the time of adoption. The adoption fee is non-refundable. The fee helps offset the cost of rescue, vetting, spay/neuter, microchipping, and care; it is an adoption fee, not a purchase price.

2. SPAY AND NEUTER
All cats are spayed or neutered prior to adoption as a standard condition of placement. In the ordinary course, the Cat has already been altered before it leaves the Rescue's care, in compliance with California law governing the release of animals by rescues and shelters.
Exception, intact placement. In the rare case the Cat is placed while still intact for documented medical or age-related reasons, the following applies: the Adopter pays a $100.00 refundable spay/neuter deposit at adoption and agrees to have the Cat altered and to provide the Rescue written proof (a spay/neuter certificate or vet record) within 30 days of the adoption date, or within 14 days of the Cat reaching the appropriate age and weight if it is a kitten placed intact.
If proof is timely provided, the deposit is refunded in full. If it is not:
(a) The $100.00 deposit is forfeited; and
(b) The Rescue reserves the right to reclaim the Cat, as an unaltered rescue cat contravenes both this Agreement and the Rescue's mission; provided, however, that
(c) If the Adopter is making a good-faith effort to comply (for example, a surgery is booked, a low-cost or voucher clinic has a waitlist, or the Cat's health has delayed the procedure), the Rescue will grant a reasonable written extension. Only refusal or non-response triggers the reclaim right.

3. INDOOR-ONLY HOME AND GENERAL CARE
The Adopter agrees to keep the Cat as an indoor-only companion animal. The Cat may access the outdoors only in a fully enclosed, escape-proof structure (such as a catio) or under direct supervision on a secure harness, with an up-to-date registered microchip and, preferably, an AirTag or GPS tracking device. The Cat shall not be allowed to roam freely outdoors and shall not be kept as a barn cat, working cat, or free-roaming outdoor cat.
The Adopter agrees to provide the Cat with proper food, fresh water, shelter, enrichment, and routine and emergency veterinary care for the life of the Cat, and to comply with all applicable state and local animal-welfare and licensing laws.
The Adopter agrees not to declaw the Cat. Declawing (onychectomy) is an amputation, not a nail trim, and is illegal in the State of California under Assembly Bill 867 (effective January 1, 2026), except where a licensed veterinarian determines the procedure is medically necessary for the Cat's own health. Declawing the Cat, in California or elsewhere, is a material breach of this Agreement.

4. OWNERSHIP, NO REHOMING, AND LIFETIME RETURN
Upon adoption and payment of the adoption fee, ownership of the Cat transfers to the Adopter, subject to the continuing conditions in this Agreement.
The Adopter agrees not to sell, give away, transfer, surrender, rehome, or abandon the Cat to any other person, family, rescue, shelter, or entity, and agrees never to surrender the Cat to a public animal shelter or pound.
If at any time, for any reason, the Adopter can no longer keep or care for the Cat, the Adopter agrees to return the Cat directly to Pawsitive Transformations. This return right has no time limit and no penalty: the Cat may come back to us at any point in its life, no questions asked, no judgment. The adoption fee is not refunded upon return.
This is a permanent safety net, not a restriction. The Cat always has a home with the Rescue. These terms exist so that no cat we place ever ends up on the street, in a shelter, or passed to a stranger. If your circumstances change, call us; we will take the Cat back.

5. MICROCHIP REGISTRATION
The Cat is microchipped. The Adopter agrees to register, or to keep registered, the microchip with the Adopter as the primary registrant and Pawsitive Transformations listed as a permanent secondary/backup contact. The Adopter agrees to keep their contact information current with the microchip registry for the life of the Cat and not to remove the Rescue as backup contact. This structure supports clear ownership and the lifetime return commitment above, consistent with California Civil Code section 1834.4 (AB 1717).

6. ADOPTER ELIGIBILITY AND RESTRICTIONS
By signing, the Adopter represents and agrees that:
• The Adopter is at least 18 years of age.
• The Cat is being adopted for the Adopter's own household, and not as a gift, surprise, or on behalf of a third party.
• If the Adopter rents or does not own their residence, their lease or landlord permits cats, and the Adopter accepts full responsibility for that representation.
• The Cat will never be used for research, experimentation, breeding, or any commercial purpose, nor sold or surrendered to any such entity.
• The Cat will not be declawed (see Section 3) and will be kept indoors (see Section 3).

7. HEALTH, TEMPERAMENT, AND NO WARRANTY
The Rescue is run by volunteers, not veterinary professionals. We share the Cat's known history and medical records in good faith, but we make no guarantee or warranty as to the Cat's health, temperament, breed, age, or future condition. Rescue cats frequently come from unknown backgrounds; conditions and behaviors can emerge that were not apparent at the time of adoption.
The Cat is adopted "as is." The Adopter accepts full responsibility for the Cat's ongoing and future veterinary care and costs from the adoption date forward. If a serious concern arises, the Rescue would always rather you contact us, and the lifetime return in Section 4 remains available, than that the Cat suffer or be abandoned.

8. RELEASE AND HOLD HARMLESS
To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Adopter releases, waives, and holds harmless Pawsitive Transformations, its directors, officers, volunteers, fosters, and agents from any and all claims, damages, injuries, losses, liabilities, or expenses of any kind arising out of or related to the Cat after the adoption date, including but not limited to bites, scratches, allergies, property damage, illness, or conditions later discovered.
The Adopter agrees that this release is binding on the Adopter and their heirs, successors, and assigns.

9. BREACH AND REMEDIES
If the Adopter materially breaches this Agreement (for example, by rehoming the Cat, allowing it to roam or breed, declawing it, or failing the spay/neuter condition in Section 2), the Rescue may, at its discretion, reclaim the Cat. The Adopter agrees to return the Cat to the Rescue on reasonable request in such circumstances. No adoption fee or deposit is refunded upon reclaim for breach.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. The Parties agree that any dispute shall be resolved in the state courts located in Riverside County, California. If the Rescue relocates its principal place of operation, venue shall be the county of the Rescue's principal office at the time the dispute arises.
If any provision of this Agreement is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. This Agreement, together with any adoption application submitted by the Adopter, is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the Cat.
